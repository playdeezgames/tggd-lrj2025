## Body

**The Blue Room of SPLORR!!**

You are a N00b in a maze of sixteen rooms, drawn on a 64 by 64 pixel screen. Eat, fight, find the seven keys and open the doors of the last room.

**HOW TO PLAY**

- Move with W A S D or the arrow keys (Q and Z work too). On a phone use the on-screen pad.
- Space (OK) confirms and drinks a potion. Backspace (BACK) opens the menu and cancels.
- Walk into moon people to hit them. They hit back, and they hit you as you walk away.
- Every step makes you hungrier. Blueberries fix that. Swords and shields wear out, so keep spares.
- Keys open the matching coloured doors. Some keys are under moon people.
- Your game is saved after every turn. Choose Continue on the menu to pick up where you left off.

**ABOUT**

Made for Low-Rez Jam 2025 in VB.NET and Blazor, then rebuilt in October 2026 in Odin and WebAssembly. The rebuild changed a few things: it saves, it has touch controls, letters work with Caps Lock, the player never starts in a room with a moon person, potions report the real heal, and miss messages fit on the screen.

The rebuild was written with Claude Code (an AI coding assistant) under the direction of the author. The fonts and sprites are third-party and credited below; the sound effects were made by the author.
